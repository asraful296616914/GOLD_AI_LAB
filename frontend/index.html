Indicator Guide

What each measurement represents

RSI

Relative Strength Index

Compares recent average gains and losses over a selected period. It is a momentum measurement, not a standalone forecast.

EMA

Exponential Moving Average

A weighted average that gives more importance to recent closing prices. EMA20 and EMA50 use different lookback periods.

MACD

Moving Average Convergence Divergence

Compares two exponential moving averages to describe momentum and the relationship between the MACD and signal lines.

BB

Bollinger Bands

Shows a moving average with upper and lower statistical bands. Band width can help describe changing volatility.

ATR

Average True Range

Measures recent price-range variability. It does not indicate whether the market will rise or fall.

OHLC

Candle Structure

Open, high, low and close values describe the range and body of each historical candle.
