Indicator Guide

What each measurement represents

  • RSI: momentum from recent average gains and losses.
  • EMA: moving average that weights recent closes more.
  • MACD: momentum from the gap between two EMAs.
  • Bollinger Bands: statistical bands that describe volatility.
  • ATR: average recent price range; used for stop loss and take profit levels.
